# Statement — Jonas Nguyen (warehouse manager)
Taken 2026-04-07

I want to lay out the timeline as I remember it, because I think it's important for the investigation to see how this all connected.

On 2026-03-15 we had a vendor in to do a firmware visit on the control system. I wasn't hands-on with the work itself, that's an engineering call, but I was on site and aware it was happening. After that visit, rooms B and E started reading about -40 on the dashboard, which obviously caught my eye straight away since that's nowhere near where those rooms should sit. I raised the -40 readings with engineering the same day, because that's the kind of thing I don't sit on, and I made it clear it needed looking at before it became a bigger problem.

On my end, I do the weekday probe round with handheld HH-1, but that only covers the rooms on the pick route, so it wouldn't have picked up an issue outside that path on a given day. That's just the nature of the round, it's not designed to catch everything, and I think that's worth the investigation noting rather than treating it as an oversight on my part.

Separately, the badge reader on room E was offline for most of the incident week, and that was already logged with facilities as a ticket, so access logging for that room wasn't reliable during that stretch, through no fault of the warehouse team.

I'll also mention that the utility ran a power test on 2026-03-14 that cut power for about 22 minutes, the day before the firmware visit, and I think the proximity of those two events deserves a proper look rather than being dismissed as coincidence.

I archived the old alert channel only after the incident, once I was confident nothing further needed to come through it.
