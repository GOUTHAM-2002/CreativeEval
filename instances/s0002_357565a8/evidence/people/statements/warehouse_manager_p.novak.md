# Statement — Priya Novak (warehouse manager)
Taken 2026-07-01

I want to lay out the timeline as I understood it at the time, because I think it matters for the sequence of who knew what and when.

On 2026-06-08 Borealis Coldchain sent someone in for a firmware visit on the controllers. Shortly after that visit, rooms C and E started showing readings of about -40 on the dashboard, which is obviously not a real temperature for those rooms and I flagged that straight away. I raised the -40 readings with engineering the same day, because that is not something I was going to sit on overnight. I don't write firmware, that is not my job, so once I'd reported it my expectation was that it would be looked at and confirmed one way or another as either a sensor fault or an actual excursion.

Separately, and I think this is relevant context rather than an excuse, the utility had run a power test the day before, on 2026-06-07, which cut power for around 27 minutes. That is the kind of thing that can rattle a controller and cause odd readings afterwards, so when the -40 numbers showed up I honestly thought it was more likely tied to that than anything else, on top of the firmware work.

On my side, my own weekday routine is the probe round with handheld HH-1, and that only covers the rooms on the pick route, so I want to be clear that room E and room C aren't rooms I'm walking every day with that unit.

Also worth noting, the badge reader on room E was down for most of that week — there's a facilities ticket open on that — so access logging for that room wasn't reliable either, which didn't help anyone piece together who'd been in and out.

I archived the old alert channel only after the incident, and in hindsight I'd rather have kept it live a bit longer, but at the time it seemed like a reasonable tidy-up.
